## 🎯 TOP 5 Lead Gen Arbitrage Plays

### Play 1: Real Estate Motivated Seller Leads
**What:** Generate leads of homeowners who need to sell → sell to investors/agents

**How to Generate Leads (Cost: $5-15 per lead):**
1. **Facebook Ads:** "We buy houses in [City] — get cash offer in 24 hours" → $5-10/lead
2. **Google Ads:** "Sell my house fast [City]" → $10-20/lead
3. **Direct Mail:** Postcards to pre-foreclosure list → $2-5/lead (lower conversion)
4. **Bandit Signs:** "We Buy Houses — Call [Number]" → $1-3/lead
5. **SEO:** "Sell my house fast [City]" landing page → $0-2/lead (long-term)

**What You Sell:**
- Homeowner name, phone, address
- Reason for selling (foreclosure, divorce, inherited, relocation)
- Estimated property value
- Motivation score (1-10)

**Buyers:** Real estate investors, wholesalers, cash buyers, agents
**Price:** $50-150 per lead (exclusive), $25-75 per lead (shared with 3 buyers)
**Payment Guarantee:** Buyers prepay for credits via Stripe. $500 buys 10 leads.

**Step-by-Step:**
1. Create landing page: "Get Cash Offer for Your House in 24 Hours"
2. Run Facebook ads targeting homeowners in specific ZIP codes ($20/day)
3. Collect: name, phone, address, reason for selling, timeline
4. Score leads by motivation (foreclosure = 10, just curious = 3)
5. Sell to investors: $500 for 10 leads via Stripe Checkout
6. Deliver leads instantly via email/SMS when they're generated

**How to Ensure Payment:**
- Stripe Checkout for credit purchases (one-time payment)
- Buyers prepay $500-2,000 for lead credits
- Leads deducted from their balance automatically
- When credits run out → access paused until they buy more
- ZERO post-payment collection

**Revenue Math:**
- Ad cost: $20/day → 4 leads/day at $5/lead
- Sell 4 leads/day at $100/lead = $400/day
- Daily profit: $400 - $20 = $380
- Monthly profit: $380 × 30 = $11,400
