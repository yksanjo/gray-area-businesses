## ⚠️ Legal Boundaries

### ✅ LEGAL (Do These):
- Scraping public data
- Selling enriched public data
- Using public information faster than others
- Lead generation and sale
- Tax optimization through legal structures
- Regulatory compliance automation
- Government incentive procurement

### ❌ ILLEGAL (Don't Do These):
- Hacking private databases
- Selling stolen data
- Insider trading (using non-public material information)
- Tax evasion (not the same as tax optimization)
- Fraudulent lead generation (fake leads)
- Impersonating government officials
- Violating explicit terms of service that prohibit scraping

### 🟡 GRAY AREA (Proceed with Caution):
- Scraping data that's public but behind login
- Selling data to competitors of scraped companies
- Aggressive marketing of lead quality
- Using company data in ways they didn't intend
- Multi-state tax structures that push legal boundaries

**When in doubt: Consult a lawyer. The $500 consultation is cheaper than a lawsuit.**
